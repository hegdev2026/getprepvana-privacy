PrepVana Privacy Policy

Your privacy, in plain words

PrepVana is built so you can prepare for interviews and exams without giving up your data. Your progress stays on your device. This policy explains what the app handles and why.

1

Summary

PrepVana ("the app") is built by hegdev. There is no account and no sign-in. Your learning progress and settings stay on your device. The app is free and shows ads from Google (see Ads). It uses no analytics or crash-reporting services of its own, and your learning data is never used for advertising.

2

What the app handles

Your learning progress (lessons read, quiz answers and scores, daily streak), items you save, the first name you may choose to type for a friendly greeting, and your preferences such as theme, language, focus track and daily goal.

3

How it is used

This information is used only inside the app: to show your progress, keep your streak, pick your daily practice, remember what you saved and apply your settings. It is never shared with advertisers or used for ad targeting or profiling.

4

Where it is stored

On Android, everything is stored in the app's private storage on your phone. In the web version, it is stored in your browser's local storage for the PrepVana site. We do not keep a copy on any server.

5

Network use

The study material is built into the app and works offline. The app may download updated learning content from our content server over a secure (HTTPS) connection; these requests contain no personal data. The app may download its typeface from Google Fonts the first time it runs. When ads are shown, the app connects to Google's ad services to load them (see Ads). When you tap Contact support, your own email app opens and you decide what to send. The web version is served by a hosting provider, which, like any website host, may keep standard technical logs such as IP addresses. In all of these cases your device's network address is visible to the service you connect to.

6

Ads

PrepVana is free because it shows ads from Google: Google AdMob in the Android app and Google AdSense on the website. To show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID (or cookies on the web), your IP address, device type and basic information about how you interact with the ads. Google handles this under its own privacy policy; see how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and some US states), you are asked for your choice before personalised ads are shown, and you can change it at any time in Settings → Ads → Ad privacy choices. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Ads are never based on your learning progress, answers or saved items, which never leave your device. Ads never appear between a question and its answer, inside a quiz or while you are reading. Ad content is limited to what is suitable for teenagers.

7

Permissions

Internet: to download content updates, fonts and ads. Advertising ID (Android): used by Google's ads SDK as described in Ads; you can reset or delete it in your phone's settings. The app does not ask for your camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos or files.

8

Security

The app only connects over secure HTTPS connections. Your data sits in the app's private storage and is protected by your device's own security, such as the screen lock and device encryption. Anyone who can unlock your phone could open the app, so keep your device locked.

9

Sharing and backups

PrepVana does not sell, rent or share your learning information with anyone. Google, as the ad provider, receives only the device and ad information described in Ads. If your phone's own backup feature is on, the operating system may include the app's data in your device backup, under your own account and settings, so your progress can come back when you change phones.

10

Deleting your data

Settings → Reset all progress deletes your learning data from the device, and Settings → Clear saved items removes your saved list. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. In the web version, clearing your browser's site data for PrepVana does the same.

11

Children

PrepVana is intended for students and early-career professionals aged 13 and above. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

12

Changes to this policy

If we add features such as optional accounts, sync, reminders or analytics in future, this policy will be updated before they are released. The date at the top will change and the new text will appear here.

13

Not a government app & official sources

PrepVana is an independent study app. It is not a government app, does not represent any government entity and does not provide government services. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any exam body, board, department or employer. Exam patterns, dates, eligibility, rules and figures change, so always confirm them on the official websites below. The same list is in the app under Settings → Official sources.
